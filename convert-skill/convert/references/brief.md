# Brief template

Copy this into `./convert/<site>/brief.md` and fill every part. Every line that will appear on the
page carries its source in brackets: `[old page]`, `[facts.md: pricing.ts]`, `[owner]`,
`[voice.md: <url>]`, `[competitor: <name>]`.

```markdown
# <Site> — brief

## Who it's for
One or two lines: the buyer, the situation they're in when they find the page.

## Their pain, in their words
- "<verbatim quote>" — <source, url>
- "<verbatim quote>" — <source, url>
- "<verbatim quote>" — <source, url>

## The one promise
<One sentence.>
Why this one: <the pain it answers + what competitors don't say>.

## Headline and sub
- Headline: <…>  [from: "<buyer phrase>" — url]
- Sub: <…>  [facts]
- Primary CTA: <words> → <the same link as before>
- Under the CTA: <the worry-removing line>  [facts]

## Objections and the answers
| Objection (their words, source) | Answer on the page | Fact it rests on |
|---|---|---|

## Section order
1. <section>: <what it says, in one line> — <why it's here (research)>
2. …

## What stays
Logo, colours (hex), type, tone, real facts, real pictures (paths), links.

## What the competitors taught
- Take: <…> (<competitor>)
- Avoid: <…> (<competitor>)
- The gap nobody fills: <…>

## Proof
- Real, usable: <…> [source]
- Missing → placeholders: `[PROOF NEEDED: …]` — what would fill it.

## Not on the page (and why)
Anything from the old page that was cut.
```
